# Progressive Web App vs Native App: Which Does Your Business Need?

Plenty of businesses want an app: somewhere customers can book, order, collect points or get updates. Then the questions start. Do we need both iPhone and Android? How long does App Store review take? What does it cost to maintain two versions?

There's another route: a progressive web app, or PWA. For most small businesses, a PWA delivers what they actually need from an app, with one version and no app store approval.

## What is a progressive web app?

A progressive web app is built with web technology but behaves like an app:

- **It installs straight from the browser,** in a tap, without going through an app store.
- **It gets an icon on the home screen** and opens full screen, without an address bar.
- **It can work offline,** at least in part, because data is stored on the device.
- **It can send web push notifications.** On iPhone and iPad, Apple added them for home screen web apps in iOS and iPadOS 16.4.
- **One codebase runs on Android, iPhone and desktop.**

## PWA vs native app: the main differences

| | PWA | Native app (App Store / Google Play) |
|---|---|---|
| Installation | From the browser, in a tap | Download from the store |
| Apple or Google review | Not needed | Required, and it takes time |
| Versions to build | One for every device | Usually two |
| Updates | Instant, no approval wait | Through the store |
| Push notifications | Yes | Yes |
| Access to advanced device features | Partial | Full |
| Discoverable in app store search | No | Yes |

## When a PWA is the right choice

- **An app for regular customers:** booking, ordering, a loyalty card or a members' club.
- **An internal tool for your team:** field forms, job reports, checklists.
- **An ordering or booking system** for a restaurant, studio or service business.
- **An MVP:** a first version of an idea, tested with real users before a bigger investment.

That's how I built [Excerly](https://shaydadon.github.io/Excerly/), an AI-powered fitness and nutrition app that installs from the browser and opens like any other app on Android and iPhone, with no app store involved.

An app without the App Store can still connect to your [small business automations](small-business-automations) and a [WhatsApp chatbot](whatsapp-chatbot), just like a website.

## Why a PWA costs less to maintain

A native app usually means two codebases, one for iPhone and one for Android, and every change has to be made and released twice. A PWA has one. A fix reaches every device at once, with no store review to wait for.

For a small business, that's often the difference between an app that keeps improving and one that gets forgotten.

## When a native app is the better choice

When the app needs deep access to the device, such as advanced Bluetooth or long-running background tasks. When people are expected to find you by searching the app store. Or when you're building a graphics-heavy game.

For most small and medium businesses, none of these apply. Their customers reach the app from a link on WhatsApp, in an email or on the website, not by browsing the store.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does a PWA work on iPhone?**
Yes. On iPhone you add it to the home screen from Safari's Share button, and it opens like any other app.

**Can a PWA become an App Store app later?**
In most cases, yes. You can start with a PWA, prove the idea works, and add a store version only if there's a real need.

**Is a PWA secure?**
Yes. It runs over HTTPS like any secure website, and your data is protected to the same standard.
